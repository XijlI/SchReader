SchReader turns any publisher's article page into a clean, Nature-like reading layout — one text
column with a fixed **Sections / Figures / References** rail — as a Chrome (MV3) extension and as an
Android app. Both hosts share one extractor, one reader page and one library store.

## What is new in 0.3.0

### Translate a selection where you read it

Select a sentence in the reading view: a small **译** button appears next to the highlight pen, and
the translation opens beside the text. Pressing **y** does the same without reaching for the mouse.

- The card shows the translation only. **Copy** puts it on the clipboard; **Save note** writes
  `原文 + 译文` into that paper's library note — and saves the paper first if you never starred it.
- It follows the text while you scroll, and it takes the size of its content: a single word gets a
  177 px card, a paragraph stops at 384 px. It uses whatever theme you are reading in.
- **Settings → Translation**: turn it off, or pick the target language (简体中文, 繁體中文, English,
  日本語, 한국어). An article that is already open follows the change without a reload.
- Long selections are split into 1,200-character pieces and stitched back together — a four-paragraph
  selection (2,974 characters) came back in 2.5 seconds in testing.
- The engine is Google's public translate endpoint: no account, no key, nothing to configure. It
  needs an internet connection (and a proxy if you are behind one).
- The reading view only — publisher pages are left exactly as they are.

### Words stay whole

Body text no longer hyphenates at line ends: `hyphens: auto` had been splitting English words across
lines, so a twenty-line abstract came out as nineteen with one word cut in half. Paragraphs are still
justified, and a long DOI or URL still breaks rather than widening the page on a phone.

### Under the hood

- New `extension/translate.js` (reader page + `android/tools/mirror.py`), and **no new permissions** —
  the endpoint answers with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`, so `host_permissions` stays owned by
  `sync_whitelist.py` and is untouched.
- The settings panel grew a Translation section for both hosts (extension modal and the app's
  Settings tab); `y` is listed with the other shortcuts.
- `y` joins the reader's keyboard map; nothing else in the parser, the library store or the sync
  layer changed.
